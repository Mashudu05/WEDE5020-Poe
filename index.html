Welcome to Olive & Oak

Olive & Oak Café is a family-owned spot serving all-day breakfast and lunch, built around locally sourced, farm-to-table ingredients. Whether you're grabbing a quick weekday lunch, settling in for a relaxed weekend breakfast, or looking for a quiet corner to study with good Wi-Fi, we've got a table for you.
