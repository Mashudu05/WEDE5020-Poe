Welcome to Olive & Oak

Olive & Oak Café is a family-owned café serving all-day breakfast and lunch, built around locally sourced, farm-to-table ingredients. Whether you are grabbing a quick weekday lunch, settling in for a relaxed weekend breakfast, or looking for a quiet corner to study with good Wi-Fi, we have a table for you.
