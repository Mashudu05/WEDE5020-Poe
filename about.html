Our Story

How We Started

Olive & Oak Café opened in 2023 as a small, family-run spot with one goal: serve honest food made from ingredients grown close to home. What began as a handful of tables and a single coffee machine has grown into a neighbourhood favourite for breakfast, lunch, and everything in between.

Today we still shop locally and cook fresh every morning. See what that looks like on our menu, or take a look at the gallery.

Our Mission

To serve honest, locally sourced food in a warm, welcoming community space, where every dish tells you exactly where it came from.

Our Vision

To become the neighbourhood’s go-to gathering spot, known for consistent quality and genuine hospitality.
